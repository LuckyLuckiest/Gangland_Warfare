# Gangland Warfare

A GTA-inspired Minecraft plugin bringing street-level gang warfare, police pursuit, and an underground economy to your server.

## Highlights

- **Gangs & territory** — form gangs, climb ranks, and fight over turf
- **Cops N Crooks** — police NPCs pursue wanted players and make arrests
- **Custom weapons & loot** — a full weapon system and loot chests
- **Persistent economy** — backed by MySQL or SQLite

Built on the [Keystone](../keystone) framework.
