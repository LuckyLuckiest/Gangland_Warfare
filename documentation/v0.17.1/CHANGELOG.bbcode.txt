[LIST]
[*][B]Gang owners who are not ops can disband again[/B] — [ICODE]/glw gang delete[/ICODE] (and [ICODE]remove[/ICODE]/[ICODE]del[/ICODE]) was op-only on live servers because [ICODE]gangland.command.gang.delete[/ICODE] was missing from [ICODE]plugin.yml[/ICODE]. [*][B]Other player commands that were op-only[/B] now default to true: [ICODE]gang rename[/ICODE], [ICODE]transfer[/ICODE], [ICODE]color[/ICODE], [ICODE]display[/ICODE], [ICODE]description[/ICODE], the whole [ICODE]gang ally[/ICODE] flow, [ICODE]/glw bank[/ICODE] (balance, deposit, withdraw, menu), [ICODE]/glw respawn[/ICODE], [ICODE]/glw wanted[/ICODE], [ICODE]/glw contact[/ICODE], [ICODE]/glw resource[/ICODE], [ICODE]/glw car[/ICODE] (list, info), [ICODE]/glw grapple[/ICODE] and [ICODE]/glw jetpack[/ICODE]. Rank gates and the [ICODE][player][/ICODE] admin forms are still enforced inside the commands.
[/LIST]
