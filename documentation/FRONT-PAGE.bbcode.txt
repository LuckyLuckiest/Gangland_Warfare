[B][SIZE=5]❓ Why Gangland Warfare?[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4][B]Gangland Warfare[/B] is not just another gun or PvP plugin.
It is a [B]complete crime ecosystem[/B] designed for GTA-style, roleplay, and competitive servers.

Players don't just fight — they:[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4] Create and manage [B]gangs[/B] with internal hierarchies and shared banks[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Control power through [B]money, reputation, and territory[/B][/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Hunt players with [B]bounties and wanted levels[/B][/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Progress through [B]levels, ranks, and loot[/B][/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Survive police pursuit or [B]don a badge and become the law[/B][/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Share the streets with [B]civilians[/B] — trade with them, rob them, or answer for it[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Drive cars, fly jetpacks, and refuel at the pump[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=5]⭐ Core Features[/SIZE][/B]

[B][SIZE=4]1.  Advanced Weapon System[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]A fully custom weapon engine entirely separate from vanilla combat, now with [B]five weapon categories[/B].[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Five weapon categories[/B] — guns, melee, incendiary, biological, and throwable — each with its own action class, YAML schema, and physics[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Fully configurable weapons via individual YAML files[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Multiple fire modes: single, burst, and full-auto with per-shot cooldowns[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Ammo types and reload mechanics[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Weapon durability that depletes with use and can be repaired[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Projectile types: bullet, flare, rocket, and spread (shotgun-style)[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Bullet gravity[/B] — shots drop over distance, rewarding range compensation[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Headshot and critical hit system with configurable chances and bonus damage[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Bullet penetration through blocks and multiple entities with damage falloff[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Ricochet off configurable surface materials[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Accuracy spread, recoil patterns, flyby and impact sounds[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Weapon modifiers: armor piercing, flat damage, block breaking, and bullet tracers[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Block regeneration[/B] — broken blocks can restore, uncrack, or stay destroyed per weapon[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Melee weapons[/B] — knives, machetes, and crowbars with range, knockback, and per-hit cooldowns[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Incendiary weapons[/B] — flamethrower-style cone fire spray that ignites everything in its arc[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Biological weapons[/B] — hold-to-charge syringe guns that apply potion effects on impact[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Throwables[/B] — grenades, molotovs, flashbangs, smoke grenades, and tomahawks with bounce physics, fuse timers, blast radius, and stackable UUIDs[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]In-world holograms for dropped weapons[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Resource pack integration for custom sounds[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]2.  Gang System[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]Gangs are the social backbone of the plugin.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4]Create a gang for a configurable fee — the creator becomes the owner[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Invite players, promote and demote members, and kick lower-ranked members[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Tiered rank hierarchy with custom permission inheritance — Recruit up to Leader[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Gang alliances with bidirectional agreement and friendly-fire protection[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Shared gang bank with a per-member contribution tracker[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Proportional bank payout when a gang is disbanded[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Customizable gang name, display name, color, and description[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Gang-restricted waypoints that act as private safe zones[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]3.  Economy & Banking[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]Every player has two separate money pools, plus a dedicated cash-drop system.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4]Personal cash balance — spent on gang creation, teleportation, bounties, and more[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Personal bank account — higher capacity vault that requires a one-time creation fee[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Death penalty: lose a configurable percentage of your carried cash (bank money is safe)[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Configurable death penalty formula — supports custom expressions and command-based execution[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Cash pickup items[/B] — drops are real items players loot, with small / medium / large stack variations configured in [B]money.yml[/B][/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Per-source drop rules[/B] — [B]Player_Kill[/B], [B]Civilian_Kill[/B], [B]Cop_Kill[/B], and more each pick a variation, amount range, and drop chance[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Custom pickup sound per variation with name validation[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Master switch: [B]Money_Drop.Enabled[/B] in [B]settings.yml[/B] disables the whole system without editing [B]money.yml[/B][/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Mob kills reward small random cash amounts[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Vault integration support for cross-plugin compatibility[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Admin economy commands to deposit, withdraw, set, or reset balances[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]4.  Bounty & Wanted System[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]A risk-vs-reward layer on top of all PvP.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4]Kill combos accumulate wanted stars — up to ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ with escalating consequences[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Each star tier spawns more cops and drains money at an increasing rate[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Stars decay over time, with higher-star players waiting longer between reductions[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Attacking a non-hostile civilian also increments the wanted level[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Dying immediately clears all wanted stars[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] Bounties can be placed by players or set by admins[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Kill bounties multiply over time for players on sustained hot streaks[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Configurable caps on maximum bounty per player[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]/glw wanted clear <player>[/B] — admin command to reset stars without killing[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]5.  Cops N Crooks[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]Fully AI-driven police NPCs powered by Citizens, sharing a unified NPC base with civilians.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4]Officers spawn in the world when a player accumulates wanted stars[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Five cop tiers — Officer, Sergeant, Lieutenant, SWAT, and Military — each with higher stats and better equipment[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4] AI state machine: Pursuit → Combat → Cuffing[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Cops navigate via the shared [B]NPC_Navigation[/B] tuning, so path-finding fixes land on cops and civilians in lock-step[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Lower-tier cops attempt to cuff and arrest; higher-tier cops skip cuffing and go lethal[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]When one cop enters combat range, all nearby cops in the group are alerted[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Cops fall back to targeting their most recent attacker when no wanted target is in range[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]3D-aware despawn — cops no longer vanish when you climb a tower or dig a pit[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Configurable cop weapon pools — vanilla items or custom Gangland weapons[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Spawner placement system with intelligent fallback for areas without placed spawners[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Cop count scales with wanted level via a configurable formula[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]CopDeathEvent[/B] fires for other listeners to react to fallen officers[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]6.  Civilians[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]A new NPC class that shares the cop AI base — wander, flee, trade, or fight.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4]Civilian types are defined in [B]civilians.yml[/B] with per-type entity, health, wearables, item pool, weapon pool, drops, and AI profile[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Friendly civilians[/B] wander and flee from danger; attacking them increments your wanted level[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Hostile civilians[/B] return fire using weapons from their pool[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Trader civilians[/B] open a custom inventory on right-click for sell/buy interactions[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Combat difficulty profiles — EASY / NORMAL / HARD / DEADLY — scale aim, reaction time, and fire rate[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Groups bind spawn points to a type with population caps, activation radius, and despawn radius[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Shared navigation tuning with cops via [B]NPC_Navigation[/B] in [B]settings.yml[/B][/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]/glw civilian …[/B] commands for manual spawn, despawn, and group control[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]7.  Jail & Detainment[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]The outcome of a successful arrest.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4]Cops that successfully cuff a player initiate the detainment sequence[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Jailed players are held at a configured jail location for a set duration[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Admins can handcuff, jail, and release players manually[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Players cuffed at logout are auto-routed to jail on rejoin[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Jail creation uses a radius check to prevent stacking duplicate jails[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Detainment integrates with the wanted system — clearing stars on arrest[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]8.  Gadgets — Cars & Jetpacks[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]A dedicated gadget module for drivable vehicles and deployable equipment.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Cars[/B] — drivable vehicles defined in [B]cars.yml[/B] with per-vehicle max speed, acceleration, and fuel capacity[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Every parked car is saved to the database (position, fuel, exhaust side) and rehydrated on boot — curb-side parking survives restarts[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Wobble animation on idle cars, shift-safe interactions, and dedicated [B]refuel[/B] / [B]defuel[/B] commands[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Jetpacks[/B] — off-hand-equipped flight gadget with ramped thrust, fuel-per-tick gliding, particle exhaust, and sound effects[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Empty jetpacks trigger a timed refuel session instead of silently cutting out mid-air[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][B]Shared fuel component[/B] — cars and jetpacks use the same [B]FuelContract[/B], so refuel items and UX are consistent across gadgets[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]9.  Loot Chests & Rewards[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]Randomized reward containers placed anywhere in the world.[/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4]Admins designate any chest, barrel, or shulker box as a loot chest[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]⏳ A countdown timer ticks down before the chest becomes openable[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Five rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Higher tiers require lockpicks or keys to access[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Loot tables contain weapons, ammo, repair materials, unique items, money, and XP[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4]Rewards also include a configurable random money and XP amount[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4]Level gating — certain tiers require a minimum player level to access[/SIZE]

[B][SIZE=5] Disclosures[/SIZE][/B]

[B][SIZE=4] Automatic Update Checker[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]On startup, the plugin automatically contacts the SpigotMC API ([B]api.spigotmc.org[/B]) every 6 hours to check whether a newer version is available. This is a read-only request — no player or server data is sent. Operators with the appropriate permission are notified in-game if an update is found.
[/SIZE]
[B][SIZE=4] Automatic Resource Pack Push[/SIZE][/B]
[SIZE=4]If the resource pack feature is enabled in [B]settings.yml[/B], the plugin automatically sends a resource pack download prompt to every player when they join. Players are directed to a URL configured by the server administrator. Players may accept or decline the prompt; declining does not prevent them from playing unless the server admin has enabled the kick-on-decline option.

⚠️ The resource pack URL and the enable/disable toggle are fully controlled by the server administrator in [B]settings.yml[/B]. Proper configuration opt-out controls will be improved in an upcoming update.[/SIZE]
