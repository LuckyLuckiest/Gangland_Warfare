[SIZE=6][B]Changed[/B][/SIZE]

[SIZE=5][B]Stars come from who saw the crime[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]A cop eyewitness radios it in.[/B] A cop who sees you commit a held crime ([ICODE]Wanted.Witnesses.Crimes[/ICODE]; the bundled file now holds [ICODE]Assault_Cop[/ICODE] and [ICODE]Kill_Cop[/ICODE] too) glows and reports it after [ICODE]Report_Seconds[/ICODE] (3). It then counts as seen by every witness still alive, and dispatch names the crime spot, never where you stand by then. Kill every witness first and the call never goes out. No cop saw it: a civilian's 911 call; nobody: no star (T-538, T-541).
[*][B]More witnesses, more heat.[/B] Each eyewitness past the first adds [ICODE]Per_Extra_Witness[/ICODE] (0.5) to the multiplier, up to [ICODE]Max_Extra_Witnesses[/ICODE] (3). A 911 call counts the caller and every civilian still watching (T-540). A held crime no longer gets the squad's hidden x1.5 (T-538).
[*][B]A cop killed unseen goes silent.[/B] No cop saw the killer or the body: no [ICODE]Officer down![/ICODE], no backup, his slot stays filled. Dispatch says [ICODE]Radio_Check[/ICODE] 10 s after his last radio check-in and [ICODE]Officer_Missing[/ICODE] 20 s after it, then sends backup toward that spot. When his squad was hunting you the kill counts then; otherwise a scene opens there for 60 s, and a cop who spots you near it counts the kill (not one spawned less than [ICODE]Spawn_Grace_Seconds[/ICODE], 3, ago: T-601). A 911 call that lands first closes the silence (T-545, T-546). Keys: [ICODE]cops.yml[/ICODE] [ICODE]Cops.Check_In[/ICODE]. A dead beat cop's station sends a beat mate to that spot at [ICODE]Officer_Missing[/ICODE]; its replacement follows once [ICODE]Respawn_Seconds[/ICODE] (30) have passed, and both hold the spot for [ICODE]Scene_Seconds[/ICODE] (60) (T-579, T-587). Only a cop who held a slot of the squad holds it while silent, and a silent Commander still leads (T-580, T-581).
[*][B]One crime, one star announcement.[/B] A crime that raises two stars at once shows one subtitle, one chat card and one siren, at the level it ends at, and dispatch names that level (T-583).
[*][B]Beat cops never appear in view.[/B] A station's beat cop never spawns at a station spot a player has in view (owner ruling R6, T-587); such a station's cop spawns at a nearby spot nobody has in view (behind a wall, or on open ground behind the players) and walks in, so a watched station is staffed as soon as such a spot exists, retried every second (T-606).
[*][B]One cop kill is one crime[/B], booked when the cop really dies, at the body: a lethal blow no longer also books [ICODE]Assault_Cop[/ICODE] (T-535), the hit cop's own sighting counts (T-536), and a hit the Defender's shield absorbs is never a kill (T-544). [ICODE]Kill_Civilian[/ICODE] is committed where the civilian died (T-550).
[*][B]Logging out forgives nothing.[/B] A report whose witness is alive when you log out lands at your next join, within [ICODE]Witnesses.Defer_Minutes[/ICODE] (10) (T-551).
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]The end of a chase[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Killing every cop never clears a star[/B] (T-537). While the cops hunt you, only an escape, an arrest, a death or a command lowers your stars. With no cop standing the search runs at the last-known spot at [ICODE]No_Cop_Inside_Speed[/ICODE] (0.25) inside the zone: leaving the zone pays, standing on the bodies does not. The first time it does, the chat says [ICODE]No units on you - leave the area[/ICODE] ([ICODE]Hud.No_Cop_Search[/ICODE], T-586).
[*][ICODE]Stand_Down[/ICODE] is heard after a wipe (T-542). Late reports name and tip the crime spot (T-543).
[/LIST]
