[SIZE=6][B]Quartermaster[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Place your own Quartermaster.[/B] [ICODE]/glw turf quartermaster[/ICODE] places or moves the Quartermaster where you stand, inside a turf your gang owns. It costs [ICODE]Powerup_Npc.Placement_Cost[/ICODE] from the gang bank (default [ICODE]5000.0[/ICODE], [ICODE]0[/ICODE] = free) and needs the rank that holds [ICODE]gangland.turf.upgrade[/ICODE]. Allies cannot place it.
[*][B]Refusals name the owner.[/B] A gangless player or a stranger is told which gang the Quartermaster serves and how to ally ([ICODE]/glw gang ally request <id>[/ICODE]).
[*][B]Hub read-out.[/B] The hub shows the guards on duty and who may spend (the owner buys, allies look).
[*][B]Gang tag.[/B] A short gang tag (1 to 4 characters) is drawn above each Quartermaster. It refreshes on an owner change or respawn, not on a gang rename.
[*][B]Staff placement.[/B] [ICODE]/glw turf powerupnpc set[/ICODE] is refused unless the staff member stands inside the selected turf.
[/LIST]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[SIZE=6][B]Guards and alerts[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Guard Orders.[/B] Slot 13 of the Quartermaster hub switches the turf between [B]Hold[/B] and [B]Defend[/B]. Hold keeps guards within [ICODE]Garrison.Hold_Radius[/ICODE] (default [ICODE]6.0[/ICODE], minimum [ICODE]3.0[/ICODE]) of their post and away from cops; the Quartermaster fights back when hit but stays within the hold radius (T-834). Defend is the default. Only the owning gang's upgrade rank can switch it; allies can look. The order is saved and resets to Defend when the turf changes hands.
[*][B]Owner alerts.[/B] Online members of the owning gang get a chat line and an action bar when a cop hits a member on the turf, a cop arrests a member on the turf (T-832), a guard falls, an outsider hits the Quartermaster, or the Quartermaster is killed. A death is told once per NPC; each kind has a per-turf cooldown ([ICODE]Owner_Alerts.Cooldown_Seconds[/ICODE], default [ICODE]60[/ICODE]). Allies and rivals hear nothing.
[*][B]Garrison panel.[/B] Shows guards on duty, the reserve against the cap ([ICODE]x/12[/ICODE]), the next wave, and "Reserve full" to the owner at the cap.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=6][B]Gangs[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Allies for everyone.[/B] Non-op players can use [ICODE]/glw gang ally[/ICODE] again: seven [ICODE]gangland.command.gang.ally*[/ICODE] nodes default to [ICODE]true[/ICODE]. The gang rank still gates each command; [ICODE]request[/ICODE], [ICODE]accept[/ICODE], [ICODE]reject[/ICODE] and [ICODE]pending[/ICODE] need the [ICODE]gangland-mail[/ICODE] module.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=6][B]Config and permissions[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]New keys.[/B] [ICODE]turf/turf_npcs.yml[/ICODE]: [ICODE]Powerup_Npc.Placement_Cost[/ICODE], [ICODE]Garrison.Hold_Radius[/ICODE], [ICODE]Owner_Alerts.Cooldown_Seconds[/ICODE]. [ICODE]turf/turf_messages.yml[/ICODE]: the [ICODE]Quartermaster.*[/ICODE] placement, hub, order and garrison keys and the four [ICODE]Alerts.*[/ICODE] keys (full list in the migration page). [ICODE]Quartermaster.Only_Owner_Or_Allies[/ICODE] is retired; copy any custom text into [ICODE]Refuse_No_Gang[/ICODE] and [ICODE]Refuse_Stranger[/ICODE].
[*][B]New permissions.[/B] [ICODE]gangland.command.turf.quartermaster[/ICODE] and the seven [ICODE]gangland.command.gang.ally*[/ICODE] nodes, all default [ICODE]true[/ICODE] and children of [ICODE]gangland.command.player[/ICODE] (negating that parent removes them too).
[*][B]Database.[/B] [ICODE]turf_garrison[/ICODE] gains a [ICODE]hold[/ICODE] column at first boot. No SQL to run.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=6][B]Fixed[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Earlier fixes included.[/B] The 0.17.1 fixes (T-307, T-279, T-825, T-826): non-op gang owners can disband and every online member is paid; the player command nodes for bank, balance, teleport, respawn, wanted, contact, filter, resource, car, grapple and jetpack default to true.
[*][B]Gang & Turf II included.[/B] The whole 0.19.0 release (waves, levels, safehouses, friendly-fire rules, fights).
[*][B]Waypoints (T-827, T-828).[/B] Players may list, inspect, select and deselect waypoints, and [ICODE]/glw waypoint select <name>[/ICODE] works by name.
[*][B]Quartermaster naming and parity (T-800, T-801, T-802).[/B] The tag, nameplate and owner-naming refusals work, and the fallback texts in code match the shipped YAML.
[*][B]Placement (T-804).[/B] A placement that cannot spawn refunds the gang, tells the player, and no longer leaves a ghost Quartermaster that reappears after a restart.
[*][B]Ally commands (T-808).[/B] Players can use [ICODE]/glw gang ally[/ICODE] without being operators.
[*][B]Staff placement (T-803).[/B] [ICODE]/glw turf powerupnpc set[/ICODE] is refused outside the selected turf.
[*][B]Garrison panel (T-822, T-823).[/B] The panel shows the reserve and the next wave, and the owner sees "Reserve full" at the cap.
[*][B]Owner alerts (T-820, T-821 partly, T-829).[/B] Owners are told when the Quartermaster is attacked or killed and when a guard falls. The death alerts now fire in the live game; they used to look up the NPC body after it had despawned.
[*][B]Hold radius (T-824).[/B] [ICODE]Garrison.Hold_Radius[/ICODE] below 3 is raised to 3, and a non-finite value falls back to the default, so a held guard no longer ignores its attacker.
[*][B]Placement cost (T-810).[/B] A non-finite [ICODE]Placement_Cost[/ICODE] no longer breaks placement.
[*][B]Arrest alert (T-832).[/B] Owners are alerted when a member is arrested on their turf (owner ruling).
[*][B]Hold leash (T-834).[/B] Under Hold the Quartermaster fights back but stays within its hold radius instead of chasing out of sight (owner ruling).
[*][B]Cop protection (T-835).[/B] Each Bartizan hit is now handled once by the turf cop-protection listener, not twice.
[*][B]Spawn log (T-833).[/B] The turf-NPC restart log reports spawned and pending-for-chunk-load counts instead of a misleading [ICODE]Spawned 0[/ICODE].
[/LIST]

[SIZE=6][B]Known issues[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*]T-805 a Quartermaster re-placed during a contest is never engaged.
[*]T-806 a Quartermaster lost on chunk unload stays gone until restart.
[*]T-807 the refusal hint names a [ICODE]gangland-mail[/ICODE] command, which is absent without that module.
[*]T-809 wording nits: alias versus gang name in hub lines, [ICODE]Paid $0[/ICODE] and [ICODE]Cost $0[/ICODE], and the gang tag does not refresh on a gang rename.

[*]T-821 guards do not engage a cop that hits the Quartermaster (the owner is told, but the guards do not answer).
[*]T-804 residual: a database error thrown during placement, after the gang is charged, loses the price.
[*]T-727 fight escrow is memory-only: a crash in the middle of a fight loses both stakes.
[*]T-707 [ICODE]Ranks.Minimum.Chat[/ICODE] and [ICODE]gangland.gang.chat[/ICODE] are not enforced on gang and ally chat.
[*]T-708 rally join has no rank gate.
[*]T-710 [ICODE]Ranks.Minimum[/ICODE] Upgrade=owner does not stop officers, who hold [ICODE]gangland.turf.upgrade[/ICODE] through the preset.
