[SIZE=6][B]Changed[/B][/SIZE]

[SIZE=5][B]The description says what the cops see[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Every worn piece is named.[/B] The radio line lists headwear, chest, legs and boots, the weapon in your hand, and on foot or in a car, for example "Suspect is masked in a carved pumpkin, wearing red jacket, armed with AK-47, on foot." Before, only a mask or the chestplate was named, and the line often read "Suspect is suspect".
[*][B]Items are named by what they are.[/B] The Bartizan item name comes first, so a police vest is a "police vest", not an "iron chestplate". Armour display names are ignored.
[*][B]The first 911 call of a chase names the real description,[/B] never "unknown suspect".
[*][B]Spanish.[/B] New [ICODE]wanted_messages_es.yml[/ICODE]; the radio line becomes "Sospechoso: ...". Item and dye words stay English.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]The disguise works as a disguise[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Recognition is live sight.[/B] A cop of the chasing group must see you now and know your look, or have watched you change. The old 1.5 s squad timer no longer switches the disguise off, lifts the pursuit range or waives the cuff gate.
[*][B][ICODE]Changes_Needed[/ICODE] (2) replaces [ICODE]Match_Needed[/ICODE].[/B] Out of sight, change two of head, chest, legs, boots and on foot / in a car and the cops stop recognising you. The weapon is described but never counts. An old [ICODE]Match_Needed[/ICODE] is ignored with a warning.
[*][B]The police outfit[/B] fools a fresh squad at once and is blown for the whole chase by a crime in it, or by a cop seeing you put it on. Taking a piece off and on again does not reset it.
[*][B]Posts are fooled too.[/B] Perimeter posts, the Marksman and standoff posts no longer see through a disguise.
[*][B]The look is taken at the crime.[/B] A crime reported through the witness gate records what you wore when you did it, not when the report lands seconds later.
[*][B]An escape keeps the description,[/B] so the post-escape search no longer re-stars any outfit.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]The cops tell you[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Chat lines, once per change,[/B] only while a cop of the chase can see you: "The cops recognise you: ...", "The cops don't recognise you.", and "In the police outfit, the cops take you for one of their own." Set a line to [ICODE]''[/ICODE] to turn it off. New debug state [ICODE]DISGUISE <player> state=seen[/ICODE].
[/LIST]
