[SIZE=6][B]Turf[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Guards in waves.[/B] Guards come in waves of 3 (at most 6 standing). Survivors and unused guards return to the reserve.
[*][B]Guard bounty, not cash.[/B] A rival who kills a guard gets half the guard price; guards drop no civilian cash.
[*][B]Guard roles.[/B] [ICODE]Garrison.Composition[/ICODE] gives guards jobs: shields block half of a frontal hit, marksmen keep range, and the commander's death makes the rest take cover.
[*][B]Local outlines.[/B] [ICODE]/glw turf show[/ICODE] and [ICODE]/glw map border[/ICODE] draw only the part within 40 blocks of you.
[*][B]Teleport guard.[/B] Gang teleports are refused near turfs under attack, into rival turf and during a turf fight.
[*][B]dynmap.[/B] Turfs appear on a Turfs layer when dynmap is installed.
[*][B]Civilian shouts.[/B] Owners hear shouts when a turf is held or lost.
[*][B]Turf create.[/B] Refused in worlds where gangs are disabled.
[/LIST]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[SIZE=6][B]Gangs[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Levels cost money.[/B] A level needs held-turf XP and a bank fee: [ICODE]/glw gang upgrade[/ICODE], then [ICODE]/glw gang upgrade confirm[/ICODE]. Set every [ICODE]Cost[/ICODE] to [ICODE]0[/ICODE] to keep automatic levelling.
[*][B]Caps are grow-only.[/B] Member, safehouse and ally caps apply to existing gangs without removing anything.
[*][B]Ranks by minimum.[/B] [ICODE]Ranks.Minimum[/ICODE] grants a feature to a rank and every rank above it.
[*][B]Safehouses and rally.[/B] [ICODE]/glw gang safehouse set|remove|list|<name>[/ICODE] and [ICODE]/glw gang rally [safehouse][/ICODE], with [ICODE]rally join[/ICODE].
[*][B]Friendly fire rules.[/B] [ICODE]/glw gang pvp on|off[/ICODE] and [ICODE]/glw gang ally pvp <gang> on|off[/ICODE] (allies fight only when both sides turn it on). Your own arrows and splash potions hurt you again; harmful potions are blocked between friends by default.
[*][B]Combat tag.[/B] A player hit by another gang is tagged for 15 seconds and cannot leave the gang or use a gang waypoint.
[*][B]Chat.[/B] [ICODE]/glw gang chat allies[/ICODE] talks to your gang and its allies. Staff can spy on gang and ally chat. The public chat tag is off by default.
[*][B]Notice.[/B] [ICODE]/glw gang notice <text|clear>[/ICODE] shows members a message when they log in.
[*][B]Statistics.[/B] Kills, deaths and assists per member; [ICODE]/glw gang profile[/ICODE]; [ICODE]/glw gang ranking[/ICODE] by kills, kdr, assists, level, members, online, turfs or fight wins.
[*][B]Bank privacy.[/B] Gang banks are private to the gang unless a player has [ICODE]gangland.gang.balance.others[/ICODE].
[*][B]Display names.[/B] Any language and hex colours; names that look alike are refused.
[*][B]Console.[/B] [ICODE]/glw gang list[/ICODE] and [ICODE]/glw gang info <gang>[/ICODE] run from the console. Staff tools live under [ICODE]/glw option gang[/ICODE], and every bank change is logged.
[*][B]Purge.[/B] Off by default. [ICODE]/glw option gang purge preview[/ICODE] lists candidates before [ICODE]run[/ICODE].
[*][B]Short labels.[/B] [ICODE]/gw[/ICODE], [ICODE]/gwc[/ICODE] and [ICODE]/gwa[/ICODE] when the gang module is installed.
[*][B]Menus.[/B] New [ICODE]player_profile[/ICODE] and [ICODE]gang_ranking[/ICODE] menus.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=6][B]Fights (new module)[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Module [ICODE]gangland-gang-fights[/ICODE][/B] (install id [ICODE]fights[/ICODE], needs [ICODE]gang[/ICODE]): challenge another gang to a fight of [ICODE]<size>[/ICODE] per side, with an optional bet that moves bank to bank.
[*][B]Commands.[/B] [ICODE]/glw gang fight propose|accept|decline|enlist|drop|info|arenas|stats[/ICODE].
[*][B]Arenas.[/B] Staff build arenas with [ICODE]/glw arena[/ICODE] (create, corner, spawn, exit, size, finish, show, list, tp, on, off, delete, reload). Arenas live in [ICODE]plugins/Gangland_Warfare/fight/arenas.yml[/ICODE].
[*][B]Command limit.[/B] During a fight a fighter may run only the fight verbs and [ICODE]Guards.Allowed_Commands[/ICODE].
[/LIST]

[SIZE=6][B]Fixes[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B]Turf waves and bounty[/B] (TF-20, TF-35, TF-46): guard waves and the bounty-share rules replace the all-at-once spend and the cash drop.
[*][B]Turf visuals[/B] (TF-43, TF-21): outlines and the map border draw only the part near you.
[*][B]Turf civilian shouts[/B] (TF-47): held and lost shouts use the civilian messages file.
[*][B]Friendly fire[/B] (GR-39, GR-40, GR-41, GR-15, CJ-43): own arrows and potions, world rules, fire and the ally switch are enforced as documented.
[*][B]Waypoint teleports[/B] (LS-35, LS-36): a cancelled or world-missing warm-up clears its timer and distance.
[*][B]Console verbs[/B] (T-227): staff gang list and info run from the console.
[*][B]Gang levels[/B] (US-43, US-08, GP-10): a cancelled level-up changes nothing; the fee is taken before the level is claimed.
[*][B]Rank seed[/B] (T-235): the preset rank seed is written atomically or healed on boot.
[*][B]Fight commands[/B] (CM-38, T-701): the fights command set and its help text are complete.
[*][B]Guard kills[/B] (T-700): a guard kill does not mint cash.
[*][B]Permissions[/B] (T-702): new parent nodes replace the flat command gates (see the migration page).
[/LIST]
