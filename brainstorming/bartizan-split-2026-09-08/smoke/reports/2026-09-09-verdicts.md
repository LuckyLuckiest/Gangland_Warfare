## 3. Open for the user

1. **Citizens-present rows were not exercised.** Drop a Citizens build for Paper 1.21.11 into `plugins/` and rerun
   `python smoke.py --deploy --rows D5,D7` (D5 = present, D7 = removed). The Jan-2024 `citizens-main 2.0.33` in
   `~/.m2` is not a usable stand-in.
2. **Design decision (README decisions log):** civilians declares `Plugins: [Bartizan]` and turf/cops depend on
   civilians, so a server without Bartizan loses civilians, turf and cops (D6 shows exactly that). Accept, or split the
   three Bartizan-touching classes into a tiny `gangland-civilians-bartizan` module so the three load without guns.
3. **In-game checks** (console cannot see them): recoil feel on 1.21.11 and on an older client through ViaBackwards
   (reflective `PacketAdapter.relativeCameraRotation`); cop/civilian fire rhythm (cadence now lives in Bartizan;
   SINGLE/BURST one click per shot/burst, AUTO hold); `weapon:`/`ammo:`/`wearable:` loot-chest and shop entries
   resolving; a grenade at a parked car damaging it exactly once; a `[WEAPON-BUY]` legacy sign still trading; jetpack
   refuel from a gasoline can; persistence — give a rifle, fire, `stop`, restart, the magazine state survives
   (`plugins/Bartizan/database/bartizan.db`, table `weapon`); `/bartizan` help, `/glw` help.
4. **bStats:** Bartizan ships plugin id `0` — register one.
5. **Test server state left behind:** Keystone 1.9.1 + Bartizan 0.1.0 + Gangland 0.9.0 (core + six modules) are
   deployed; your previous jars are parked in `plugins/Gangland_Warfare/modules/.harness-parked/` (and the Keystone
