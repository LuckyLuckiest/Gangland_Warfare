### 1. CHANGELOG.md line 23 and CHANGELOG.bbcode.txt line 20: Incorrect sample-cap claim for learning tables

**Finding:** Despite the previous round fixing the docket reference, the sample-cap description remained wrong: "Two tables track habits (one row per player who finished a chase) and level stats (one per peak star level), capped at 100 samples each." This falsely claims both tables are capped at 100. The truth per PLAN 3.5 is: only the server-level `typicalCount` (the sample count of `S.typical`, the learned typical chase length) is capped at 100 via `min(100, S.typicalCount + w)`. Player habit rows have no per-table cap and are pruned only by age (90 days, `Forget_After_Days`).

**Fix:** Rewrote in both files to clarify:
- **Original:** "Two tables track habits (one row per player who finished a chase) and level stats (one per peak star level), capped at 100 samples each."
- **Fixed:** "Two tables track habits (one row per player who finished a chase) and level stats (one row per peak star level, with the server-wide typical-length sample capped at 100)."

This makes clear that only the server-level sample count carries the 100 cap, not the player habit table.

**Verification:** Manual inspection against PLAN section 3.5 learning rule and section 4.2(b) defaults; no cap mentioned for `Learning` keys other than the 100-sample implicit in `typicalCount` logic.
