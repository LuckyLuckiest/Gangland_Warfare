## 4. "A chase today" five-star walkthrough, after 0.15

1. **You take out a rival**: CHANGED. No more "two kills in 10 s". Each kill is a heat crime (Kill_Player 80; x1.5 streak bonus on a repeat inside the 10 s window; x1.5 if a cop saw it; x0.5 inside a contested turf); 100 heat = 1 star, so the second quick kill (or a witnessed one) gives the first star. Exempt (no crime): self-defence, defending your own contested turf, an ally/gangmate, your own arrow. Needs `Heat.Enable` (false = old combo/one-star-per-kill math).
2. **You drop a second one / first star**: spawn rule UNCHANGED (about a second later two cops, same spawner logic, radio dispatch, 0.13 roles). NEW: star card title "<Crime>: <Tier> inbound, they want you in cuffs / they shoot first", siren, red boss bar; the chat line is replaced.
3. **You punch an officer**: CHANGED. Hitting a cop is now a crime: Assault_Cop 100 = a star (once per attacker+cop per 10 s); the squad still learns your position and fights. A killed cop adds Kill_Cop 150, so a one-shot cop kill lands at 2 stars (owner question).
4. **You wipe the squad**: CHANGED. Two cops down within 20 s makes the rest pull back to cover for 15 s ("Two down! Pull back to cover, backup is coming!"), a backup is granted regardless of the backup cooldown, then "Backup's here! All units, push together!". The spawn task still refills when the fall-back ends; `CopDeathEvent` is now published and the kills feed the ledger.
5. **You reach five stars**: stars now come from heat (thresholds 100/250/450/700/1000); squad size 2/4/6 at 1/3/5 stars and the mixed composition are UNCHANGED (acceptance R1). New: HUD (card, siren, bar) at each step.
6. **You hide in a basement**: CHANGED. Evasion replaces the fixed timer: unseen for 3 s starts a countdown in a search zone (radius 40/60/90/130/180 by stars), 10/20/30/45/60 s per star, one star at a time (ONE_STAR); being seen resets it; the bar flashes yellow with the countdown, the zone ring and compass show the area; leaving the zone runs the clock 2x. Repeating_Timer stays as the safety net when no cop is alive. Money: nothing is taken per star by default (`Take_Money.Enable false`; with it on the default formula is still 50 x 5^stars, $156,250 at five). The 0.13 stuck-cop recycle is UNCHANGED.
7. **You jump off a roof**: MOSTLY UNCHANGED. Death still resets stars (cause DEATH) and costs the hospital bill `balance * 0.15`; changes: a broken formula falls back to 15% with one warning instead of throwing, the bill is clamped to the wallet, and Lose_Money false means no bill at all (it used to pay you). Bill moves to respawn in 0.16. Notoriety is not cleared by death (owner question).
