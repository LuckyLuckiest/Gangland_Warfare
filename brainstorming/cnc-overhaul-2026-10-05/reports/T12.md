## Summary
JailIntakeService.admit reads the level and `ledger.chaseCrimes` before the ARREST clear; unless the player is dead
or the sheet is disabled it charges min(wallet, fine) rounded down to cents (wallet only), turns fine - paid into
capped extra sentence seconds, stores finePaid/fineExtraSeconds on the DetainedPlayer, prints SHEET_HEADER, one
SHEET_CRIME per distinct crime (first-seen order, counts), SHEET_TOTAL, SHEET_PAID, SHEET_EXTRA when extra > 0.
Two nullable columns fine_paid / fine_extra_seconds on detainment (table, getData, doLoadAll cursor, SQLite rebuild
DDL AND columnsToCopy). PaperworkView gains the package-private static `fineLine` and appends it to the info lore.

## Rulings
- Ruling: existing DetainmentRepositoryMigrationTest legacy DDL now includes fine_paid/fine_extra_seconds - at runtime
  `RepositoryRegistry.createTables` applies the schema diff (adds the columns) BEFORE `migrateSchema`, so the rebuild
  always sees them; a legacy fixture without them models a state that cannot occur - cost if wrong: none for runtime,
  but a table where the diff failed would make the rebuild throw (same as any other column).
- Ruling: SHEET_PAID is sent even when paid is 0 (broke player), a fine of 0 sends nothing and sets no columns - the
  contract says "SHEET_PAID" unconditionally and "a fine of 0 sends nothing" - cost: one cosmetic line.
- Ruling: tests stub sentence cost as 120 + 90 x stars (300 at 2 stars) to reproduce the Done-when 180 + 120 + 40 = 340
  without the real cost config.
- Ruling: PaperworkView gets `WantedMessages` as a new last constructor field (bean takes it as a parameter) - required
  for the fineLine seam; cost: none.
