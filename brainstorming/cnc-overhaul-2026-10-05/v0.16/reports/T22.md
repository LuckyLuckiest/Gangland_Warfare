## Summary - root cause per symptom
1. **Radio line never heard (0/3) - PRODUCT BUG, fixed.** `HandoffController` spoke `Handoff` through `CopRadio.sayFromLeader(group, key, extra)`,
   which speaks from the leader's body with no addressee (`SquadRadio.speak` delivers within `Range` 32 of the speaker, or to the hunted
   player within `Target_Range` 64). The hand-off fires only when a RETURNING cop is beyond `Pursuit.Max_Distance` 80 and every chaser has
   left the squad (`ReturningBehavior.onEnter` -> `leaveSquad`), so the speaker is the leaderless fallback cop, 80+ blocks away: out of
   range by construction. Fix: new overload `CopRadio.sayFromLeader(group, key, extra, @Nullable LivingEntity addressee)` (the 3-arg form
   delegates with null); the controller passes the suspect, so the line is also heard within `Range` of him. Evidence: in the pass1 S6 run
   the bot heard the dispatch line (spoken around the target) one second after HANDOFF, but not the hand-off line.
2. **HANDOFF fired in 2 of 3 runs - PRODUCT BUG (one cause) + HARNESS RACE (another). The failing run's own log was overwritten, so which
   one hit run 1 is inferred, not proven.**
   - The `:105-111` target check is NOT the cause: `CopManager.resolveTarget` keeps a wanted target on every cop each AI tick, RETURNING
     included; only `onWantedEnd` or a stale (offline/dead/downed/unwanted) target clears it.
   - Product: `isEngaged` counted a POSTED cop wherever it stood. Losing sight of a runner fires SEARCHING -> `PerimeterController.start`
     (posts up to 2 cops and leaves at least one chasing) BEFORE the chasers reach the 80-block leash (sight 40 < leash 80), so a post left
     on the ring where he was lost kept the group "engaged" for up to `Perimeter.Max_Seconds` 60 and the hand-off never fired in the
     window. Pass1 shows it on the second wave: `Eddie PURSUING -> POSTED` and `PERIMETER start posts=1` in the same second Jim went
     RETURNING, then no further hand-off. Whether SEARCHING precedes the last leash is a timing race, consistent with 1 run in 3.
     Fix: a POSTED cop counts as engaged only within `Pursuit.Max_Distance` of the suspect (the same `farFrom` rule as the leash); a post
     near him still holds the chase (`postedCopKeepsTheGroupEngaged` unchanged and green).
   - Harness: S6 waited 20 s standing next to the arriving squad. In the last run a cop entered CUFFING 7 s after spawning and was
     interrupted only because the hops began 4 s later (a cuff takes about 5 s). A completed cuff restrains him and correctly ends the
     chase with no hand-off. Fixed in the scenario: the run starts 3 s after the first UNIT line.
3. **`bias=true ahead=false`, `hidden=false` on every biased unit - HARNESS GEOMETRY, no product change.** The sandbox world is flat and
   empty: `EntitySpawner.isOutOfSight` (Keystone 1.15.0) needs a block between the suspect's eye and the spot, so `hiddenRing` finds
   nothing and C8 step 4 runs (`spawnNearPlayer` -> phase-1 ring BEHIND the player's facing; the hops keep yaw 0 = south, so units land
   north/west of him). That is what the contract specifies (PLAN risk 5), already pinned by
   `CopSpawnManagerUnitTest.nothingHidden_legacyPath`; the ahead filter itself by `activeBias_ringSpotIsAhead`. Fixed in the scenario: two
   3-high stone walls along the run (x 300..700 at z 94 and z 106, forceloaded, removed at the end of the row) so hidden ring spots exist
   ahead of him; the verdict grades `ahead=true` on hidden biased units and reports a biased unit on the fallback (`hidden=false`) as
   REVIEW, not FAIL.
