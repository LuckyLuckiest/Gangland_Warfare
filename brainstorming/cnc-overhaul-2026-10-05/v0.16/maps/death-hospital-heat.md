## Spec drift (spec statement vs today's code)
1. Line refs: spec `PlayerDeathListener.java:81-85, 91-114, 116-119, 131-157, 213-228` are stale. Death path is :82-86, downed :104-114, threshold check :119 inside `handleCommandExecution` (:117-130), charge `handleMoney` :133-160, class ends :212. ":213-228 broken formula throws" is OBSOLETE: MoneyFormula (0.15.0) makes it safe, `handleMoney` has no throw path.
2. "Threshold check at :116-119" is not a standalone check: it is `handleCommandExecution`'s early return, which is also the command-execution gate. A planner moving the bill must keep the (`balance <= Threshold` => skip) AND the `Command.Enable` branch, or re-home both.
3. "Charged on the downed path ... moves to respawn": today it is charged at `PlayerDownedEvent` (LOWEST) in PlayerDeathListener, i.e. when downed. `performRespawn` has no money hook, and quit-while-downed has no charge at all today.
