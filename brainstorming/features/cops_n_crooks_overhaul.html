Cops N Crooks Overhaul

Right now the cheapest way to lose five stars is to jump off a roof. This plan makes the police something you outplay. Crimes need a witness, units arrive from a station, stars fade only when you break line of sight, and whatever you get away with follows you afterwards.

Where 0.11.0 stands

A chase today

The police AI is in good shape. Squads share what they see, route around buildings and climb after you. The rules around the AI are what make chases feel flat. Here is a five-star chase played by the current code.

  1. You take out a rival. Nothing happens. The first star needs two kills inside 10 seconds, and every victim is worth one point, whether it was a player or a cop.KillCombo.recordKill passes int points = 1 · Kill_Counter: [2, 5, 10, 15, 20]

  2. You drop a second one. One star. Two Officers appear about a second later, 10 to 50 blocks away or at a spawner within 80, already heading for you.CopManager.onWantedStart · startSpawnTask

  3. You punch an officer. The whole group turns on you. Your stars don't move, because hitting a cop is not a crime.CopManager.onCopAttackedAlert

  4. You wipe the squad. It's back at full strength on the next spawn check, two seconds later.startSpawnTask refills every missing cop in one pass

  5. You reach five stars. Six Military NPCs. Each star is one tier in bigger numbers, and nothing new ever shows up.CopSpawnManager.getTierForWantedLevel

  6. You hide in a basement. It makes no difference. A star drops every 193 seconds whether anyone can see you or not, and each drop takes 50 × 5stars cash, which is 156,250 at five stars.WantedExecutor · Wanted.Take_Money

  7. You jump off a roof. Every star is gone for 15% of your cash. Waiting it out would take about 16 minutes and 195,250, so dying is the smart play.WantedLevelListener.onPlayerDowned · User.Death.Money.Formula: "balance * 0.15"

  8. Or they catch you. Up to eight minutes in a cell under Slowness IV and Blindness, with nothing to do.DetainmentService.applyVisuals runs for the cuffed and jailed states alike

Also missing

  • Kills are the only crime. Robbery, car theft, assault, gunfire, safe cracking and trespassing raise nothing. The only other sources are [WANTED] signs and admin commands.
  • A pedestrian kill adds a star on the spot through a second listener (CivilianDeathRewardListener), and that star never starts the decay timer. That one is a bug worth fixing on its own.
  • Heat has no HUD. Stars show up in chat lines and placeholders, with no boss bar, action bar or title.

Already working, and worth keeping

  • Squad awareness. Cops share sightings, chase what they've seen, and search outward from where you were last seen.

  • Route planning. Doors, ladders, rooftops searched from the top down, and a patient wait below anything unreachable.

  • Tiers with teeth. Difficulty profiles from EASY to DEADLY, Bartizan weapons, and ranged cops who hold a 7 to 12 block firing band.

  • A full arrest pipeline. Cuffs, transit, jail, handcuff bribes, break-free, bail, jail bribes and sentences, all released through one ReleasePipeline.

  • Living civilians. Faction squads that alert each other, and cops that already hunt hostile civilians.

  • Events to hang things on. WantedStartEvent, WantedLevelChangeEvent, CopDeathEvent, CuffedEvent and KillComboEvent are ready for new listeners.

The biggest lever is already built

Every AI tick, each squad knows whether anyone has seen you in the last 1.5 seconds and where you were last seen (NpcSquad.hasFreshSighting()). The cops use it to choose between chasing and searching. Nothing connects it to your stars. Wiring the two together turns escaping into a skill, so it is the first thing on the roadmap.

The target

The chase loop

Each feature below fits one step of this loop, and the stage numbers follow the same order. Stage 6 then puts players on the police side of it.

  1. 1

    Crime Stage 1

    Heat is weighted, so a punch, a robbery and a dead cop are different things. It only counts once a cop, a chopper, a dog or a witness sees it.

  2. 2

    Response Stage 2

    Witnesses call it in. The nearest patrol answers first, then units leave the closest station. Each star adds a new kind of unit.

  3. 3

    Pursuit and search Stage 3

    Seen

    Stars stay solid. The squad closes in and calls out your position.

    ⇄
    Unseen

    Stars flash. A search zone forms around your last known position and the evasion clock runs.

  4. 4

    Ending Stage 4

    • Escaped
    • Surrendered
    • Busted
    • Wasted

    Every ending gets a pursuit report, and none of them is free.

  5. 5

    Record Stage 5

    What you escaped stays on your record as a bounty. Posters go up, hunters ride out, and cops in that district recognize you on sight.

    ↺ Back to step 1. A recognized player needs no witness.

Escalation

What each star means

Today each star is one tier in bigger numbers, and a star drops on a fixed clock. The proposal gives every star its own mix of units and something new to deal with, and lets you shake it by staying out of sight. Treat the numbers as starting values to tune.

The crime

More crimes worth committing, weighed by how serious they are, and counted only when someone sees them.

Headline

Heat ledger

  • GTA V
  • RDR2
Effort
S
Ships in
0.12

Every crime pays out heat, and heat turns into stars. Shooting a cop lights you up at once. Shoving a stranger barely registers. Crimes chained inside a few seconds still stack up fast, so the kill combo keeps its bite.

Replace the one-point-per-kill combo with a table of crime weights. Star thresholds move from kill counts to heat, and the combo window becomes a streak bonus. Crimes a cop sees skip any report delay. Routing pedestrian kills through the ledger also retires the separate, timer-less star in CivilianDeathRewardListener.

Give players reasons to get wanted besides killing. Each of these ends in a chase if someone sees it.

The response

Units come from somewhere, and every star puts something new on screen.

Headline

Dispatch from stations

  • GTA V
Effort
M
Ships in
0.13

The first response comes from whichever patrol is nearby. Everyone else leaves the nearest station, and the radio tells you they're coming. A crime next to a precinct is answered at once. A crime out in the hills buys you time.

Squad members spawn at the nearest station (a cop spawner) and travel from there. With no station in range, they appear beyond the player's view after a delay based on distance. Stage 3 · Getting away

The chase

Stars fade when you break line of sight and stay out of it. Everything in this stage helps players do that, or read how close they are.

Headline

Line-of-sight evasion and the search zone

  • GTA V
  • GTA IV
Effort
M
Ships in
0.12

While any cop, chopper or dog has eyes on you, your stars stay solid. Break line of sight and they start to flash as a search zone forms around the last place you were seen. Stay unseen and the clock runs. Get out of the zone and it runs faster. When it finishes you lose a star. Get spotted and the zone snaps back onto you.

The squad already knows, every AI tick, whether anyone saw you in the last 1.5 seconds and where. Tie that to the stars: the repeating decay timer becomes an evasion clock that only runs while the squad has no fresh sighting. The old timer stays as a safety net for chases where no cop can spawn. The per-star cash drain goes away (see fines and bills).

Sketch for the wanted config

Wanted:
   Evasion:
      Enable: true
      Lost_Sight_Seconds: 3                  # stars flash after this long unseen
      Drop_Mode: ONE_STAR                    # ALL_STARS plays by GTA V rules
      Search_Radius: [40, 60, 90, 130, 180]  # blocks, per star
      Seconds_To_Drop: [10, 20, 30, 45, 60]  # unseen time, per star
      Outside_Zone_Speed: 2.0
      Hideout_Speed: 1.5
   Take_Money:
      Amount: 0                              # no more chase tax

A clean five-star escape takes about three minutes of staying unseen (10 + 20 + 30 + 45 + 60 seconds), less if you leave each zone. Today it takes 16 minutes and 195,250 cash, or one death.

Bounty hunter license

Effort

Ships in

Buy a license, take contracts from the bounty board, and track your mark with a compass that points at their last reported position. When they go down, cuff them before they respawn. Alive pays in full, dead pays half.

Contracts come from the record (players over the threshold) and from the hostile civilians the police already hunt. The downed state is the capture window, the way hogtying works in Red Dead. Deliver the prisoner to a jail to get paid.

Cops 'n' Crooks event

  • GTA IV
Effort
L
Ships in
0.16

A scheduled server event based on GTA IV's multiplayer mode of the same name. One gang's boss has to reach a getaway point across the map, and the police, on-duty players plus NPC units, have ten minutes to stop them. The cops see the boss's search zone. The crooks see nothing.

An event scheduler builds two teams from a gang and the duty roster, picks an extraction waypoint, and pays out to the gang bank or the police payroll. Breaking news covers it live.

Constraints

Guardrails

Rules for every feature

  • Behind a toggle. Each feature gets its own Enable key in the module's own config, which is where the module API rules put new settings.

  • Stock APIs only. Boss bars, per-player particles, map renderers, compass targets and Citizens NPCs cover everything here. The core jar ships no NMS, and this plan doesn't need any.

  • Inside the AI budget. Choppers, dogs and roadblock cops count toward Max_Per_Player, and their sight checks run on the existing AI_Tick_Rate of 10 ticks.

  • Fair PvP. No bounty payouts between gang mates or allies, a cooldown on switching sides, no clocking in while wanted, and a per-target cooldown on hunter contracts.

  • Additive API. New events such as CrimeCommittedEvent, WitnessReportEvent and EvasionStateChangeEvent are additions, which gangland-api allows within a major version.

Decisions for you

  • One star at a time, or all at once? GTA V clears every star when you evade. The plan drops one star per clock and leaves Drop_Mode to switch.

  • Should death still wipe stars? GTA says yes and RDR2 keeps the bounty. The plan does both: death clears the stars and the record stays.

  • Do turf wars draw the police? The plan halves heat for kills inside a contested turf. Zero would keep turf fights police-free.

  • Police as a faction or a shift? The plan uses shifts anyone can clock into, so nobody is locked out of crime for good.
