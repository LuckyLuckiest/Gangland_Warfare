Verified end to end: (1) WeaponReloadListener only listens to PlayerItemHeldEvent (hotbar-slot switch) to call stopReloading(); it has no InventoryClickEvent/InventoryDragEvent/InventoryOpenEvent handler at all (grep of bartizan-plugin confirms the only two InventoryClickEvent listeners in the whole plugin are WmItemConverterListener and WearableEquipListener, neither touches reload state).
