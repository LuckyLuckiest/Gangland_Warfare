Gangland Decoupling Wave

Six features leave the Gangland core: the scoreboard becomes its own plugin, menus move onto a new keystone-inventory module (re-targeted from Oriel on 2026-09-17, ruling R10), loot chests and the gang system become runtime modules, holograms and the shop system move into Keystone, and gangland-api grows a Bartizan-style facade. Planned by six Sonnet planners, attacked by Opus reviewers, consolidated here. Sources: brainstorming/decoupling-wave-2026-09-14/ (PLAN.md, plans/, reviews/, census/). Added 2026-09-16 (gadget wave, brainstorming/gadget-wave-2026-09-16/): WS7 brings the jetpack home from Bartizan and makes Bartizan a soft dependency of the gadget module; WS8 adds the grappling hook and a gadget shortlist. Both run first, on Gangland 0.9.2.

What Keystone ships (E6, 1.11.0)

The basic mechanism both plugins duplicate today, sourced from Oriel's menu-core/chest with Gangland's unique pieces folded in: holder-identified menus (MenuHolder, the MenuTarget seam), one MenuListener for click/drag/close/quit that the consumer registers (the keystone-hologram precedent — Keystone still registers nothing itself), ClickKind resolution and SlotHandlers, Menu/Component/SlotView/RenderContext/CloseReason, a trimmed ChestMenu + ChestMenuBuilder, item/fill/border/line components, a per-consumer InventoryService (open-menu tracker + name registry, BeanLifecycle, no static state), MenuFlow/Panel/FlowState reconciled with MultiPanelInventory (in-place switch, suspend/resume), and PageConfig + PagedRegion. Six consumers (shop-api, lootchest-api, npc-shops, turf, cops-n-crooks, gadget) use only InventoryHandler + Fill + InventoryUtil + flow.* and move mechanically to ChestMenuBuilder, the four components and MenuFlow. The trader barter and sell views keep today's interactive-slot behaviour (SlotView.interactive is Gangland's draggable), so they are re-points, not the deposit-slot redesigns the Oriel plan needed.

Why the jetpack is in Bartizan today

Before 0.9.0 the jetpack was modelled as a wearable (a chestplate entry with a Jetpack: block in the weapon module's wearables.yml). The Bartizan split ruled "Bartizan owns wearables", so the jetpack's item identity and configuration moved to Bartizan's items/wearables.yml (as generic Extra_Tags:) while the flight behaviour stayed in gangland-gadget. The gadget module therefore hard-depends on Bartizan (Plugins: [Bartizan], fail-fast) only to know what a jetpack is.
